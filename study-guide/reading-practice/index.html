The art of looking closer.

Three short reading activities to use with any book you have with you.

Original learning activities. Keep your own book beside you; no book excerpts are reproduced here.

01
5–10 minutes

Notice the small things

Slow down. Let a detail catch your attention.

↗02
10–15 minutes

Follow an idea

Find the thread between one moment and another.

↗03
10–15 minutes

Make room for another view

Bring your reading into conversation.

↗

A little preparation.

Choose a short passage. Keep a pencil or your reading notebook nearby. Read at a pace that gives you room to think.

Follow the path in order, or choose the activity that interests you. There’s no score and no timer.
