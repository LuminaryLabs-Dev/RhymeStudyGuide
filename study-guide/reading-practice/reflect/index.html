Reading practice 03 · 10–15 minutes

Make room for another view

Bring your reading into conversation.

Use with your own book · Original reading activity

On this page

Begin hereThink about itLook closerYour reflection

Choose a moment that could be understood in more than one way. Think about it on your own, then invite another perspective.

01

Name your first impression

Write what you think is happening and why. Separate what the text says from what you infer.

02

Try another viewpoint

Imagine how another character or reader might understand the same moment. What would they notice?

03

Return to the text

Compare interpretations using specific details. You can revise your thinking without needing a single final answer.

Think about it

Let a question lead.

Open a question for a gentle nudge. There isn’t a single required answer.

01Whose perspective shapes this moment?+

Consider who speaks, who observes, and whose thoughts you cannot hear.

02What would change your mind?+

Identify the kind of evidence that would challenge your first impression.

03What will you take into your next reading?+

Choose a question, an idea, or a habit of attention to return to.
