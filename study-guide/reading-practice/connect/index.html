Reading practice 02 · 10–15 minutes

Follow an idea

Find the thread between one moment and another.

Use with your own book · Original reading activity

On this page

Begin hereThink about itLook closerYour reflection

Choose two moments from your reading that seem connected. They might share an image, a choice, a question, or a feeling.

01

Find the first thread

Describe the first moment in your own words. Note a page number so you can find it again.

02

Look for an echo

Find a later moment that repeats, challenges, or changes the first. Similarities and differences both matter.

03

Make a connection

Explain what the two moments suggest together. Use a detail from each to support your thinking.

Think about it

Let a question lead.

Open a question for a gentle nudge. There isn’t a single required answer.

01What changes between these moments?+

Compare the character’s choices, the language, or the mood—not just what happens.

02What idea holds them together?+

Start with a topic, then turn it into a question the story seems to explore.

03Could someone read the connection differently?+

Imagine another interpretation. What evidence would support it?

Look closer

Give your idea a little room.
