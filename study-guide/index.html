Read. Notice. Discover.

A guided way to look closer, connect ideas, and reflect on your reading.

A companion,
not a test.

Bring any book you’re reading. Choose a short passage, slow down, and let a question guide you. You don’t need to finish an activity in one sitting.

These original reading-practice activities work with your own book. They do not quote or summarize Dylan’s books.
