About Rhyme Study Guide

More than finishing a chapter.

Discover the purpose of Rhyme Study Guide and how to use the reading materials.

Read with room to think.

This guide supports a simple habit: return to a detail, ask a better question, and see what changes. It welcomes independent readers, families, teachers, and reading groups.

Start with what you’re reading.

The reading-practice activities, general themes, vocabulary definitions, and discussion prompts can be used with any book. They are original learning materials, not excerpts, summaries, or confirmed interpretations of Dylan’s books.

Keep your own pace.

Activities have no scores or deadlines. Your reflections can stay in this browser, or you can use a printable sheet. The site does not send your notes to a server.

A comfortable reading space.

You can turn off decorative motion using the Motion button. Your device’s reduced-motion preference is respected. Reading material and navigation remain available without animation.
