The word companion

Words open worlds.

A small companion to the language of reading: definitions, examples, and questions.

A small glossary of reading terms. Browse at your own pace.

C

Character

A person, animal, or other being who takes part in a story.

↗I

Imagery

Descriptive language that evokes a sensory experience, such as a sight, sound, or texture.

↗I

Inference

An interpretation drawn from evidence and reasoning rather than a direct statement.

↗N

Narrator

The voice that tells a story. The narrator is not necessarily the author.

↗R

Rhyme

A repetition of similar sounds, often at the ends of words or lines.

↗R

Rhythm

The pattern of stressed and unstressed sounds, pauses, and movement in language.

↗S

Setting

The place, time, and surrounding conditions in which a story happens.

↗T

Theme

An idea or question developed through a work’s events, language, and relationships.
