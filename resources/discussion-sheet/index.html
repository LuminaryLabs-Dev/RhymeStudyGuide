A conversation worth having.

A printable discussion guide for your next reading conversation.

Choose a passage together. Take a quiet moment to think. Invite each reader to share or pass.

Notice

Which small detail would you ask another reader to look at again?

Return to the passage together. What do you each see?

Interpret

When did your understanding of a character change?

Find the moment that shifted your thinking. Compare your interpretations.

Connect

What connects the beginning of your reading to the end?

Look for a repeated image, question, or choice.

Reflect

What question would you carry into the next chapter?

Explain why it matters to you. Listen for a question you had not considered.

Imagine

How would this scene change if someone else told it?

Retell a few sentences from another point of view.

Close with one question you would like to return to.
